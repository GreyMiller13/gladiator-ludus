## Стиль

```text
Art style: stylized 2D game illustration for a strategy game about running a Roman gladiator school (1st century AD). Hand-painted digital art with clean dark-brown ink outlines and soft cel shading with a light painterly texture. Slightly stylized, readable shapes with realistic proportions. Warm earthy palette: sand, ochre, terracotta, bronze, olive, deep Roman red, muted natural skin tones. Historically grounded Roman details. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no frame, no modern objects.
```

## Портреты

```text
Composition: head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter view, the face turned slightly to the right of the viewer, eyes looking toward the viewer. The head is centered and fills about 45% of the image height; all important details stay inside a centered circle (the game crops the portrait into a round bronze-rimmed medallion), nothing important near the corners. Background: plain dark warm-brown backdrop with a soft vignette, no scenery. Lighting: warm soft key light from the upper left. Square image.
```

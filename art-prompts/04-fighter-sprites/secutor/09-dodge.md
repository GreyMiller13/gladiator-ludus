# Секутор — уклонение

**Где в игре:** анимация бойца-секутор на поле боя
**Формат:** квадрат 1:1, 1024×1024, та же рамка, что у листа персонажа
**Референс:** приложите `secutor_00_reference.png`
**Сохранить как:** `secutor_09_dodge.png`

## Промпт

```text
Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference. Draw the same gladiator again: identical body, face, skin, hair, helmet, armor, weapon, shield and colors, the same scale and the same art style. Keep the same fixed framing: facing right, feet on the invisible ground line at 92% of the image height, perfectly flat solid chroma-key green (#00FF00) background, no ground, no shadow, no other objects. New pose: dodging: leaning back and sidestepping away from a blow coming from the right, back arched, light on the feet.
```

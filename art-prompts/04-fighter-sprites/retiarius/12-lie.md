# Ретиарий — повержен

**Где в игре:** анимация бойца-ретиарий на поле боя
**Формат:** квадрат 1:1, 1024×1024, та же рамка, что у листа персонажа
**Референс:** приложите `retiarius_00_reference.png`
**Сохранить как:** `retiarius_12_lie.png`

## Промпт

```text
Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference. Draw the same gladiator again: identical body, face, skin, hair, helmet, armor, weapon, shield and colors, the same scale and the same art style. Keep the same fixed framing: facing right, feet on the invisible ground line at 92% of the image height, perfectly flat solid chroma-key green (#00FF00) background, no ground, no shadow, no other objects. New pose: defeated: lying on his back on the ground, horizontal, the body resting on the same ground line, head toward the left, weapon dropped beside him, exhausted or unconscious.
```

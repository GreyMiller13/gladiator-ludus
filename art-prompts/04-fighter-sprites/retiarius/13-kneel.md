**Референс:** приложите `retiarius_00_reference.png`
**Сохранить как:** `retiarius_13_kneel.png`

Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference. Draw the same gladiator again: identical body, face, skin, hair, helmet, armor, weapon, shield and colors, the same scale and the same art style. Keep the same fixed framing: facing right, feet on the invisible ground line at 92% of the image height, perfectly flat solid chroma-key green (#00FF00) background, no ground, no shadow, no other objects. New pose: begging for mercy (ad digitum): kneeling on one knee, weapon and shield put down on the ground, raising the left index finger toward the crowd, head bowed.
